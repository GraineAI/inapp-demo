# Graine Store — products, prices and how deliveries work

Graine Store is a fictional daily-essentials store used in the Graine demo apps. Everything below is made up
for the demo and is consistent with the store's agents (Tara and the freshness desk) and with the app's
Deliveries screen. Upload it (as `knowledge.pdf`, which `scripts/` can print from this file) as the agents'
knowledge base so they can answer questions about products, prices and rules.

## How deliveries work

- Graine Store delivers every morning, seven days a week, including Sundays and public holidays, between
  5:30 am and 7:30 am. Each customer has a one-hour delivery slot inside that window, chosen when they sign up.
- Deliveries are no-contact. The delivery partner leaves the items where the customer asked: a bag on the door
  handle, the doormat, or the society's security desk. The bell is rung only if the customer asked for it.
- Changes for tomorrow — skip, pause, resume, add or remove an item — can be made until 11 pm tonight. A change
  made after 11 pm applies from the day after tomorrow.
- A delivery slot can be changed once a week, to any free slot between 5:30 am and 7:30 am. The change applies
  from the next delivery day.
- Graine Store delivers in Delhi, Jaipur and Bengaluru.

## Subscriptions

- A subscription is a product delivered on a schedule: every day, every other day, or on chosen days of the week
  (for example every Sunday).
- Pausing a subscription stops it until the customer resumes it. It restarts on the day after they resume
  (or the day after tomorrow, if they resume after 11 pm). There is no charge for pausing.
- Skipping stops only tomorrow's delivery of that product; the subscription carries on after that.
- Going away: pause every subscription before the trip and resume them when back. Tara can pause and resume them
  in the chat, or the customer can do it on the Deliveries screen.
- Quantity can be changed at any time; the new quantity applies from the next delivery after 11 pm tonight.

## One-time add-ons

- Add-ons are delivered once, with tomorrow's milk, and paid from the wallet. They can be added until 11 pm and
  removed until 11 pm.
- Bread is baked overnight, paneer is made the day before delivery, and fruit is picked to be ripe the next day.

## The wallet

- Graine Store is prepaid: every delivery is paid from the wallet on the morning it is delivered.
- A delivery goes out only if the wallet covers it. When the balance falls below ₹100, the customer is reminded to
  top up before 11 pm so that the next morning's delivery is not held.
- Top-ups are made in the app's wallet, by UPI, card or net banking. The store's assistants never take card,
  UPI or bank details in a chat or call, and cannot top up for the customer.
- Auto top-up adds a fixed amount (for example ₹500) when the balance falls below a level the customer chooses.
- Refunds are always made to the wallet.

## The freshness promise

- Milk is packed the night before delivery and kept below 4 °C until it reaches the door.
- If a product is sour, spoilt, leaking or wrong, or a delivery does not come, the customer reports it the same
  day — in the app, or by telling Tara or the freshness desk. The price of that product (or of the whole
  delivery, if it did not come) is refunded to the wallet within 24 hours. No photo is needed.
- A refund that has not arrived after 24 hours is looked into by the freshness desk, which can call the customer
  at a time they book.

## Questions customers often ask

- **Can I get milk only on weekdays?** Yes: choose the days for the subscription, for example Monday to Friday.
- **I'm going away for a week. What should I do?** Ask Tara to pause your subscriptions, or pause them on the
  Deliveries screen, before 11 pm the night before you leave. Resume them when you're back.
- **Can I change tomorrow's order at midnight?** No. Changes for tomorrow close at 11 pm; after that they apply
  from the day after tomorrow.
- **My milk was sour. What happens now?** Report it today. The price is refunded to your wallet within 24 hours,
  and tomorrow's delivery goes out as usual.
- **My delivery didn't come.** Report it today. The amount charged is refunded to your wallet within 24 hours.
  Tell us if anything about your doorstep changed, such as a new gate at the society.
- **Can someone call me?** Yes. Book a call with Tara (Calls from Tara) or with the freshness desk (Help), at a
  time between 8 am and 9 pm.
